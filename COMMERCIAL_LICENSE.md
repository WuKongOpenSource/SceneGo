# SceneGo Commercial License (MCL)

SceneGo is offered through two alternative licensing paths:

1. the public community edition under `AGPL-3.0-only`; or
2. a separately negotiated and signed SceneGo Commercial License (`MCL`).

The MCL path is required when an organization needs proprietary commercial
terms, such as distributing closed-source modifications, providing a hosted
service without fulfilling the AGPL source-availability obligations, embedding
SceneGo in a closed product, or obtaining separately contracted implementation
and support services.

The AGPL does not prohibit commercial activity. A use that fully complies with
the AGPL may continue under that license. The MCL is the alternative path for
commercial use that needs rights or terms outside the AGPL.

`MCL` in this repository means the project-specific **SceneGo Commercial
License**. It does not refer to any third-party license or template that may use
the same abbreviation.

This file does not itself grant commercial rights or contain the MCL terms.
