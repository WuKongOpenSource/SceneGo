# Third-party notices

SceneGo uses third-party packages and assets that remain subject to their own
licenses and attribution requirements. The project license does not replace or
override those terms.

The exact dependency versions are recorded in the committed Python and npm lock
files. Supplementary license evidence for packages whose distribution archives
do not carry a standalone license file is under
`docs/open-source/third-party/`. The scope and remaining evidence limits are
described in `docs/open-source/dependency-review.zh-CN.md`.

Before redistributing a build, review the complete resolved dependency graph,
the license files shipped with the installed packages, and the notices produced
by the toolchain for the target platform. Native, operating-system, FFmpeg,
font, icon, model, and provider terms may differ by deployment.
